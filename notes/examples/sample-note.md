# Sample Note

This is an example of a note organized in a subdirectory.

## Why Organize Notes?

Organizing notes in subdirectories helps you:
- Keep related topics together
- Find information quickly
- Maintain a clean structure as your notes grow

## Example Structure

You might organize your notes like:
- `work/` - Work-related notes
- `personal/` - Personal notes
- `learning/` - Educational content
- `projects/` - Project documentation

Feel free to create your own structure that works for you!
